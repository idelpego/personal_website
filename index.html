Performer - Technician - Activist

Isolde Emilio Del Pego is a versatile performing artist and costume technician from San Diego. He began his training at Idyllwild Arts as a Dramatic Arts major, building a strong foundation in acting and dance. He is currently pursuing a BFA in Musical Theatre at Chico State Theatre (CST), where he continues to develop both his performance skills and his growing expertise in theatrical costuming. At CST, he works as a costume stitcher and as a member of the wardrobe teams.

Isolde is passionate about theatrical activism and community engagement, and he strives to create work that sparks dialogue and connection. One of his favorite performance experiences has been playing Ernst Ludwig in Cabaret, where he had the opportunity to engage with a powerful and historically resonant story.

Supporting Artists and Building Community

As a committed advocate for community theatre and accessible public art, Isolde upholds a vision of a humane and equitable society grounded in universal participation in artistic creation. He attended Idyllwild Arts Academy, where he developed a deep love not only for creating but for witnessing the creative expression of others. Because of this, Isolde believes that the foundation of any strong community lies in mutual respect for each person's passions and in the belief that your voice can be heard, whether on the page of a book, on a stage, or within your government.

Theatrical Activism

Isolde is committed to using his creative work to help progressive and emerging productions reach broad and diverse audiences. A strong advocate for anti-recidivism prison practices, LGBTQ+ rights, and mindful consumerism, he believes deeply in empowering audiences through feminist storytelling, queer-centered narratives, and works that model strength through empathy.
